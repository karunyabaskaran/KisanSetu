# Crops Cultivated in India — State-wise Guide
### Fruits, Vegetables, Rice, Cereals/Millets, Pulses, Spices & Herbs
**Reference month for prices: September 2026**

---

## How to read this document

- **Major growing states** are listed in rough order of production share (largest first), based on standard Indian agricultural geography (APEDA, Ministry of Agriculture crop-production data). Many crops are grown in smaller quantities in other states too — only the leading states are listed for readability.
- **Price/kg** is an **indicative all-India average retail price** for September 2026. Vegetable/fruit prices in India swing sharply week to week and city to city (a crop can double in price within days due to rain, festivals, or supply gaps), so treat these as **rough reference points, not quotes**. Where I could not find or reliably estimate current data, the cell is left **blank** as requested.
- Prices for spices/herbs sold in small bunches (coriander, mint, curry leaves) are natively priced **per bunch**, not per kg, in most local markets — noted where relevant.
- This list aims to be broad ("almost everything" commercially grown), but India has thousands of local/heirloom varieties — extremely minor or hyper-local crops are not all individually listed.

## Notes on data limitations

1. **Vegetable & fruit prices** move daily; the figures above use a September 2026 Chennai wholesale reference (Koyambedu market) plus typical national retail ranges, and should be treated as **indicative**, not exact for every city.
2. **Spice/dry-commodity prices** (turmeric, pepper, cardamom, cumin, etc.) are approximate wholesale/retail ranges based on typical 2025–26 market levels; actual September 2026 mandi rates can be checked live at **Agmarknet** (agmarknet.gov.in) or the **Consumer Affairs Ministry price monitoring portal** for exact, city-wise, daily figures.
3. Blank cells mean I did not have reliable current data and did not want to guess a number.
4. State lists reflect **typical leading producers**, not an exhaustive list of every state where a crop is grown in small quantities.
